Below deck

What the engine does for you

The exact rules are ours to keep. Here's what they add up to.

A watch every minute

Every account is checked every 60 seconds, around the clock. Good prices don't wait for a slow schedule.

Buys that lower your cost

It adds to a position only when the new price improves your average. Dips become opportunities.

Sells only in profit

A position closes only when it's ahead after every pool fee. It never sells just because the price dipped.

Gentle on the pools

Big orders are cut into slices, so we never push the price against ourselves.

Never stuck on one node

Each trade is signed once and offered to several Hive nodes in turn. A busy node doesn't cost you a trade.

Books that match the chain

Every trade is confirmed against your real on-chain balance before it counts. Your ledger and the blockchain always agree.

The lighthouse

Seven safeguards keep watch

Hive-Engine has seen pools drain overnight and bridged tokens lose their footing. HiveHarbor was built with those nights in mind.

Only deep water

We trade a hand-picked set of Hive-Engine's deepest pools. A pool that gets too shallow is closed to new buys.

A way out before every buy

Before each buy, the engine stress-tests whether every client together could still sell out cheaply, even if the pool shrank sharply. If not, it doesn't buy.

Reads the pool, not the panic

It tells an investor quietly withdrawing liquidity apart from a real sell-off. One just makes trades smaller; the other stops new buys at once.

Bridged coins checked against the world

Tokens like SWAP.BTC are compared with live market prices. If one drifts away from its real coin, buying stops.

Spots sudden token floods

A sudden jump in a token's supply pauses that pair before new coins can water down your position.

No single pair can sink the ship

Each pair may hold only a small part of your account. Even if a pair failed completely, the damage stays contained.

A human on the bridge

A troubled pair is first watched, then paused, then frozen. Only the operator can unfreeze it, and an emergency exit at a loss happens only on hard evidence and his own confirmation. Everything else, the engine handles by itself.

    Before you board

    Questions from the dock

    Do I hand over my funds?

    No. Your SWAP.HIVE stays in a Hive account that's yours, and you keep its owner key. The engine trades from that account and sends your share out of it.

    Why is it invite-only?

    Every account trades in the same pools, and we size everything so all clients together can always get out cheaply. That only works with a carefully kept circle.

    What does it cost?

    10% of realised profit, nothing else. No fee on deposits, no monthly fee, nothing while a trade is waiting.

    How do I get paid?

    40% of each profit is sent to the Hive account you choose, as SWAP.HIVE. Small amounts are gathered and sent together, so your wallet isn't flooded with dust.

    Can I leave?

    Yes. It's your account and your owner key. Tell us, and your account comes off the engine.

    Which tokens does it trade?

    A short list of deep Hive-Engine pools paired with SWAP.HIVE, chosen for liquidity and watched every hour. A pair joins the list only after it has proven itself.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. Crypto prices move and pools can fail, and you can lose money. HiveHarbor is built to manage those risks, not to remove them. Only deposit what you're comfortable leaving to work.
